Employment and Community First (ECF) CHOICES

ECF CHOICES is a long-term services and supports program.

Eligibility

  • Individuals with intellectual or developmental disabilities
  • Meets medical and functional criteria

Covered services

  • Home and community-based services
  • Employment support
  • Transportation supports

Provider information

  • Providers must be enrolled and compliant with TennCare requirements
  • Training and credentialing may be required

How to apply

Contact TennCare or your local agency to start the application.
