# GrantFlow competitive feature matrix — 2026-08-25

This matrix records the closest five products found in official-site research and maps their strongest capabilities to concrete GrantFlow implementation and test evidence. The order reflects functional similarity to GrantFlow's discovery-to-award purpose, not market share. It is an engineering evidence map, not a claim that GrantFlow is fully production-ready.

## Portal-adapter interpretation

The built-in `backend/services/portalAdapters/` registry contains four legacy families: external applications, university financial-aid portals, scholarship portals, and manual/offline applications. That compatibility path is intentionally draft/handoff-only; the canonical Hamilton browser orchestrator is the sole authority for a real external submission. `backend/tests/portalAdapterConfirmationContract.test.js` now enumerates every built-in adapter and portal type, verifies identity/attestation stops remain intact, and prevents any legacy adapter from manufacturing a submitted outcome or confirmation value.

Official pages were retrieved with Firecrawl on 2026-08-25. Product behavior and counts can change; future updates should re-check the linked primary sources and update this dated artifact rather than silently overwriting its evidence boundary.
