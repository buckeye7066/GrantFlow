# Profile Sections: Organization vs Individual Focus

When filling profiles, some sections are more relevant to organizations (e.g., biotech companies, nonprofits) and others to individuals (e.g., students, families seeking assistance).

## Sections Most Relevant to Organizations

| Section | Use for org profiles |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Basic Information** | Name, contact, address — always fill |
| **Organization Details** | EIN, UEI, mission, budget, staff — critical for grants |
| **Nonprofit Compliance** | 501(c)(3), SAM.gov, fiscal sponsor — **turn these ON** if your org is registered; required for federal grants |
| **Location Focus** | State, ZIP, geographic focus — enables local/state grants |
| **Programs & Services** | Focus areas, keywords — high-signal for crawler matching |
| **Narrative** | Mission, goals, target population — used by AI matching |
| **Financial Information** | Budget, funding amount needed — eligibility signals |

## Sections That Can Apply to Both

| Section | Org use | Individual use |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Military Status** | Veteran-owned business, military contractor | Veteran benefits, military spouse scholarships |
| **Demographics** | N/A (org-level) | Race, ethnicity, disability — scholarship/assistance eligibility |

## Sections Primarily Individual-Focused

These are optional for organization profiles. Filling them won't hurt but may not unlock additional grants for a biotech/research org:

| Section | Typical use |
|---------|-------------|
| **Housing** | Housing assistance, homelessness programs, broadband |
| **Household Details** | Household size, support system |
| **Family & Life Situation** | Single parent, foster youth, caregiver, disaster survivor |
| **Medical Insurance** | Individual healthcare assistance programs |
| **Medical History & Needs** | Medical assistance, DME, copay support |

## For Biotech/Research Orgs (e.g., Axiom Biolabs)

**Critical to fill:**
- Basic Information, Organization Details, Location Focus, Programs & Services
- **Nonprofit Compliance**: Turn ON 501(c)(3), SAM.gov, Fiscal Sponsor if applicable

**Optional but helpful:**
- Military Status (if veteran-owned or military-related research)
- Narrative (mission, goals, target population)

**Low priority for org profiles:**
- Housing, Household Details, Family & Life, Medical Insurance, Medical History
