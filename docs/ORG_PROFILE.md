# Your Organization profile (GrantFlow)

GrantFlow lets you set up your organization once, so it can later find grants
that fit you. There is no account to create and nothing to configure.

## Running the app

1. Install what the app needs: `npm install`
2. Start the app: `npm run dev`, then open the address it prints (usually
   http://localhost:5173) in your web browser.
3. To run the automatic checks: `npm test`

## What happens when you use it

- The first time, you'll see a welcome line and one big **Set up your
  organization** button. Click it to open the form.
- Fill in whatever you like. **Every field is optional**, so you can save with
  as much or as little as you want.
- When you click **Save**, you'll see the message *"Saved. We'll use this to
  find grants that fit you."* and a tidy summary card.
- Come back later and your summary is right there, with an **Edit** button to
  change anything.

## Where your information is kept

Your organization details are saved **on this device, in this web browser
only**. Nothing is sent over the internet and no account is needed. If you
clear your browser data, or open the app in a different browser or on another
device, the saved details won't be there.

If saving ever fails (for example, in a private browsing window), you'll see a
plain message that says *"We couldn't save your changes. Please try again."*
and nothing you typed is lost — just try Save again.
